Questions people ask before cleaning up

Can I delete multiple ChatGPT chats at once?

Not in ChatGPT itself. It deletes one conversation at a time from the three-dot menu, or every conversation at once from Settings → Data controls → Delete all chats. There is no checkbox multi-select.

Chat Cleanup adds the missing middle: checkboxes, Shift-click ranges and age filters, so you can delete a specific batch and keep the rest.

How do I delete all my ChatGPT chats?

If you want everything gone you do not need an extension: Settings → Data controls → Delete all chats. It is permanent.

Use Chat Cleanup when you want most of your history gone but not all of it — say everything older than a year, while pinned and Project chats stay.

Can I delete old ChatGPT chats but keep the important ones?

Yes — that is the reason this extension exists. Age presets select everything older than 30, 90, 180 or 365 days, and pinned chats, Project chats and chats you have marked protected are skipped automatically. You can still include a protected chat by selecting it explicitly, and the confirmation says so.

What is the difference between archiving and deleting a ChatGPT chat?

Archiving removes a conversation from your sidebar but keeps it in your account, restorable from ChatGPT settings. Deleting removes it from your account and cannot be undone.

If you are not sure, archive. Chat Cleanup does both.

Can a deleted ChatGPT conversation be recovered?

No. Once deleted it is gone from your account and Chat Cleanup cannot bring it back; OpenAI's own retention policy governs what happens on their servers afterwards. That is why every destructive batch shows you the exact list first.

Is a ChatGPT bulk delete extension safe to install?

It depends entirely on the extension — anything that can read your chat list could in principle send it somewhere. Chat Cleanup has no backend, no account, no analytics and no third-party requests. It asks for exactly two permissions, storage and access to chatgpt.com, which Chrome shows you before you install and which you can re-check any time on the extension's page.

Does it work with ChatGPT Projects?

Yes. Conversations inside Projects are loaded and can be expanded and selected as a group. They are excluded from global and rule-based selection by default, and only conversations are ever changed — never the Project itself.

What happens if I close the tab in the middle of a cleanup?

The unfinished batch is stored locally in your Chrome profile. Reopen ChatGPT and you can resume or discard it. A request whose outcome was uncertain is reconciled by reading its result before it could ever be sent twice.

Does deleting old chats make ChatGPT faster?

No, and it is worth being clear about that. Response speed depends on the length of the conversation you currently have open, not on how many old chats sit in your sidebar — starting a fresh chat is what helps there.

Cleaning up makes the sidebar usable and your history easier to search. It does not make ChatGPT think faster.

Can I export my ChatGPT chats before deleting them?

Yes, and it is worth doing before a large delete. In ChatGPT open Settings → Data controls → Export data; a download link arrives by email.

Chat Cleanup does not export anything itself. It only archives and deletes, which is why archiving is the safer choice when you are unsure.

Is there a limit on how many chats I can delete at once?

No. Chat Cleanup is free and puts no cap on how many conversations you select, unlike free tiers that limit you to a handful per batch.

Large batches simply run at a deliberate pace: archive processes one conversation at a time and delete at most two, so ChatGPT's rate limits are respected and a stopped batch can be resumed.

Which browser does Chat Cleanup work in?

It is built and tested for Google Chrome and is distributed through the Chrome Web Store. You need to be signed in to ChatGPT in the same browser.

Is Chat Cleanup free, and is it affiliated with OpenAI?

It is free. It is an independent extension and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by OpenAI.
